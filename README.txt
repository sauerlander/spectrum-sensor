A six channel double light sensor is desrcibed, based on the AS7262 chip. This sensor measures light intensity, separated in six wavelength channels. The channels are 450, 500, 550, 570, 600 and 650 nm with full width at half maximum of 40 nm. Both sensors are of identical design. They are housed in an aluminium casing and fitted with a plexiglass cover. The plexiglass has a satin finish to achieve an approximate cosine correction. They have been tested to a diving depth of 30 metres.
